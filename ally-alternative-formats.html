Ally Alternative Formats

Listen to a reading, use an ebook format, or try another way to access your course materials.

Available to any student. You do not need an accommodation to use the Alternative Formats options offered in your course.

Find it. Choose it. Download it.

  1. Find it: Look for the Alternative Formats icon beside a file in Blackboard Course Content. For documents created directly in Blackboard, open the document and look in the top-right corner. Its label may read Download Alternative Formats.
  2. Choose it: Open the menu and select a format that suits your task.
  3. Download it: Follow the download prompt, then open the file with an app that supports that format.

See Ally’s full student guide and video (opens in a new tab)

Which format should I try?
  • Audio: Listen to the text.
  • ePub: Read in an ebook app, including on a phone or tablet.
  • Semantic HTML: Read a version that adjusts to your screen.
  • Tagged PDF: Try a PDF with structure to support assistive technology.

Your available choices depend on the original content; you may see other formats or fewer options.

I don’t see the option—or the result is hard to use

Alternative Formats may not be enabled for the item, or its content type may not be supported. Tell your instructor which item you need and what is difficult to access.

If a converted version leaves something unclear, check the original and ask for help. You should not have to guess at important information.

Need accessibility support?

Alternative Formats can be useful for everyday study. If you need disability-related accommodations, contact OCTC Accessibility Services (opens in a new tab) to learn about requesting support.

Using Ally does not replace the accommodations request process.

Having a technical problem?

Contact OCTC IT at 270-686-4541 and leave a voicemail if no one answers, or email octc-ithelpdesk@kctcs.edu. Include the course, file name, and error message or screenshot. Never send your password.
